

# Pamgolet Sales and Profit Analysis

## Project Overview

This project features a comprehensive **Power BI Dashboard** designed to analyze the sales performance and profitability of Pamgolet across various global markets, products, and customer segments for the years **2013 and 2014**. The goal of this analysis is to identify growth drivers, evaluate regional performance, and pinpoint areas of financial risk.

## Key Business Questions Addressed

* How has sales volume changed year-over-year?
* Which products are driving the most revenue and profit?
* Which geographical markets are most lucrative for the company?
* Which customer segments are underperforming or resulting in losses?

## Data Insights & Key Takeaways

### 1. Sales Performance

* **Rapid Growth:** Total units sold increased by **200%**, growing from 175 units in 2013 to 525 units in 2014.


* **Product Leadership:** **Paseo** is the clear market leader, selling **338K units**—far exceeding competitors like VTT (169K) and Velo (162K).


* **Peak Sales:** The highest revenue month recorded was **July**, with Paseo generating over **$1.15M** in that month alone.

### 2. Profitability Analysis

* **Top Earner:** Paseo remains the most profitable product, achieving a total profit of **$4.80M**.


* **Geographical Strength:** The **United States** is the strongest market, contributing over **$25M** in total sales. Profitability is also strong and well-distributed across Canada, France, and Germany.


* **Risk Area:** The **Enterprise segment** is currently a "loss leader," reporting a total loss of **$614.55K**.

## Strategic Recommendations

Based on the dashboard insights, the following actions are recommended:

1. **Scale Success:** Increase marketing and distribution for **Paseo** in top-performing countries (U.S., Canada, France).


2. **Optimize Government Relations:** Prioritize the **Government segment**, as it delivers the highest returns ($11.4M profit).


3. **Address Enterprise Losses:** Conduct a deep-dive review of the Enterprise segment's pricing and cost structure to reverse the current negative profitability.

## Technical Features

* **Data Modeling:** Integrated multi-year sales data to create year-over-year (YoY) comparisons.


* **Interactive Visuals:**
* **Donut Charts:** For YoY unit distribution.


* **Bar/Horizontal Charts:** For ranking product sales and segment profitability.


* **KPI Tiles:** To highlight "Highest Profit by Product" and "Highest Sales by Country" for immediate executive summary.


* **Geospatial Analysis:** Comparative analysis of sales performance across five key global markets.



## Tools Used

* **Power BI Desktop:** For data transformation, DAX calculations, and dashboard visualization.
* **Data Source:** Pamgolet Sales and Profit dataset (2013–2014).
